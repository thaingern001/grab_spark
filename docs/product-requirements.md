# Grab One Click — product requirements (Phase 1)

Status: revised Phase 1 design, approved for Phase 2 backend implementation on 2026-11-07. This is an **Unofficial Concept Prototype — Synthetic Data**. No Grab production integration or real merchant action is implied.

## Outcome and scope

The demo shows two independent experiences over one merchant dataset: **One Click reduces learning friction; AI Connect reduces switching friction.** One Click guides a small restaurant owner through **Daily Opportunity → One-Click Review → Approve → Track Results**. AI Connect lets an authorized external MCP client read the same analytics and mission history. The backend, rather than either client, owns arithmetic, access checks, and state changes.

The merchant is Westrong Kitchen, a fictional small Thai restaurant. Currency is THB; business timezone is `Asia/Bangkok`. The demo uses deterministic synthetic records. Every screen and response that could be confused with actual business data bears a synthetic-data label. Mission trial results also say **Simulated outcome**.

## Terms shown to merchants

* **Sales / gross merchandise value (GMV):** item selling prices before discounts and deductions, excluding any delivery charge and tax (neither is modeled in v1).
* **Merchant revenue:** GMV less merchant-funded discount and modeled platform deduction.
* **Estimated contribution:** merchant revenue less modeled variable food, packaging, and other per-order costs. It is not net profit; fixed expenses, tax, volume changes, and substitution are outside the estimate.
* **Today's AOV:** today's GMV divided by today's completed, non-refunded orders. Empty periods display “No orders” rather than zero AOV.

## Guardrails

One Click approval changes only prototype mission records. Menu management is read-only in v1 except scenario cost verification. There is no promotion publishing, price update, customer information, order placement, or production API connection. MCP offers no mutation tools. Simulated results are never mixed into historical observed-order aggregates.

## Acceptance measures

Record first recommendation review completion, interactions and elapsed time from opportunity open to approval, comprehension of contribution versus profit, confidence in the recommendation, authorized MCP connection/tool completion, and REST/MCP consistency. Adoption, retention, GMV uplift, and profit improvement are future pilot KPIs, not prototype results.

## Proposed defaults and approval questions

Defaults: one merchant, one authenticated demo user, one proposed bundle mission, one completed simulated mission; the demo date is fixed at 2026-11-07 for repeatability. Percent deductions apply to pre-discount item price. A simulated trial runs for seven days after approval, with deterministic outcome generated by an explicit demo action. Product approval is needed for the synthetic fee basis, fixed-THB versus percentage discount, fixed demo date, and whether remote MCP beyond Inspector is necessary. These are design proposals, not approved product or real-world financial terms.
# One Click multi-strategy requirement (Phase 3 extension)

Home opens a GrabMerchant-inspired One Click bottom sheet for a proposed mission. It displays backend-evaluated Bundle, Discount, and Advertising cases, each with seven-day contribution difference and a synthetic 0–100 risk score. The lowest-risk eligible case receives the recommendation. The merchant can inspect financial details, risk dimensions, assumptions, and costs, and edits must recalculate through backend services. The shortest path is one informed approval from the sheet after two explicit confirmations. Approval persists an immutable evaluated strategy snapshot. Processing generates an immediate seven-day synthetic result, and Results & Learning displays the approved strategy, baseline/trial contribution, difference, risk, assumptions, and limitations. Dismissal returns to Home's recommendation entry point. No production campaign is created.
